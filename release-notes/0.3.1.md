# MooMole 0.3.1

## English

- Sidebar labels show their full names. Page filters, dropdowns and system status cards have more consistent spacing and alignment.
- Dialogs use a consistent title and close button, with a separate scrolling area for long content. The device backup dialog keeps its title visible and gives actions and warnings more room.
- Quick Look refresh history displays one result per row, with its time and completion count. Related maintenance actions are grouped together.
- Space Monitoring shows the latest reading and trend together, with controls for recording intervals and low-space thresholds below. Folder schedules and login controls are easier to scan.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Choose `arm64` for Apple Silicon or `x64` for Intel. Verify the DMG size and SHA-512, quit the old app, then copy MooMole from the DMG to Applications. Packages use ad-hoc signing. See [Apple's first-open guidance](https://support.apple.com/102445) if macOS blocks opening.

MooMole is an independent, unofficial project for learning and research. Review each operation and back up important data. Thanks to [Mole CLI](https://github.com/tw93/Mole) and its contributors. The bundled engine is 1.65.0. See the [official Mole product](https://mole.fit/). Matching source and dependency materials accompany both installers under their respective licenses.
